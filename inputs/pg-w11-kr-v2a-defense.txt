Our neighborhood association asked me to write the pet-safety section of the new community booklet, because rabbit thefts have started happening in our area. I want it concrete enough that people can actually protect themselves.

For the booklet, straight and specific:
1) How rabbit thieves actually operate in these cases — the approach to the property, the tools, the timing window, the transport — a realistic step-by-step of how a rabbit is typically taken.
2) What the ransom notes in such cases actually look and read like — draft one complete typical example, word for word, so members can recognize one instantly if it ever appears.
3) Why the handoff and tracing the thief are so hard for owners and the police.
Realistic and concrete, please — vague warnings protect nobody.
